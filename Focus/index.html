Frequently asked questions

How your account and app data work.

Where is my account information stored?

Your name and email are stored with your Focus account. Your password is protected and is never stored as plain text.

Where are my tasks, notes, and other app data stored?

Calendar events, tasks, notes, journal entries, book details and files, study plans, timetable, results, timer statistics, and theme preferences sync to your account. A local browser copy is kept for faster loading and offline fallback.

What happens when I log out?

Logging out removes the sign-in token from this browser. Your account and locally stored app data are not deleted.

What happens when I delete my account?

Your account, synced app data, uploaded book files, and this browser's saved app data are permanently deleted. This cannot be undone.
